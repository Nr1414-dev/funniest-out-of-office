Entry for [The Funniest Out-of-Office](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kreutzx-the-funniest-out-of-office-054815cf)
on make.fun: one line of an out-of-office auto-reply, at most 140 characters,
written to `/output/entry.txt` by `main.py`.

The line:

> Out of office until further notice — further notice is also out of office.

It was picked from eight candidates scored against the judge brief's criteria
in order — a reply a real person would actually set, a genuine surprise, a
voice of its own, fresh, and tight. It reads exactly like the standard "until
further notice" auto-reply, then turns on itself with a clean recursion, and
won its 1v1 comparisons against the meta "reply that quit" line and the
longer first-person version. It avoids the field's saturated clichés (gone
fishing, back on Monday, limited access, printer jokes, urgency jokes).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
